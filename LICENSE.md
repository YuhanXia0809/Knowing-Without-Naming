## Upstream material

Upstream sources keep their own licences and terms.

- English definitions come from WordNet (Princeton University), whose
  licence permits redistribution with attribution; they ship in full.
- Chinese source definitions come from the Ministry of Education, Taiwan,
  *Revised Mandarin Chinese Dictionary* (moedict `dict-revised.json`),
  licensed CC BY-ND 3.0 TW (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nd/3.0/tw/). They ship verbatim, unmodified and with
  attribution, in `data/moedict_source_zh.jsonl`, which remains under
  CC BY-ND 3.0 TW. Our two adaptations of that text (the OpenCC
  Traditional-to-Simplified conversion and the answer-avoiding paraphrase)
  are not distributed; only their sha256 digests are, so they can be
  rebuilt locally and checked.
- The SUBTLEX, age-of-acquisition, concreteness and CLEARPOND norms (and
  the other per-item values drawn from upstream sources) ship only as
  per-item values, under their providers' terms, not as
  redistributed databases.
- wordfreq (Speer, 2022; code Apache-2.0, bundled frequency data
  CC BY-SA 4.0) supplies the `zipf` and `freq_per_million` values of the
  English beyond-corpus pool and the Chinese hard pool and of the sets
  drawn from them. Those values remain under CC BY-SA 4.0
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- The `en_equiv` field of the Chinese item files (481 of the 903 Chinese
  items of the main dataset carry it) reproduces CC-CEDICT glosses and
  remains under the CC-CEDICT licence, Creative Commons
  Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 (CC BY-SA 4.0)
  (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
- The per-item records contain text generated by third-party language
  models; the terms of the respective model licences may apply to it.
